AI Hub privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

AI Hub is a personal desktop app for Windows, used by its owner on their own computer. This page describes what it does with Google user data.

What AI Hub asks Google for

  • Send email and save drafts (Gmail permission gmail.compose). AI Hub sends scheduled results only to the email address its owner typed into the app, and only if that is the Google account that signed in. It can save an email in the owner's Gmail Drafts instead of sending it.
  • Read message details, not contents (Gmail permission gmail.metadata). To confirm that an email it sent arrived in Sent, AI Hub reads one header that it added to that email. It doesn't read the body or attachments of any email.
  • The account's address, to check that it matches the address the owner typed.

Where the data stays

  • The Google sign-in is stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted for their Windows account. AI Hub has no server; nothing is sent to its developer.
  • Nothing read from Gmail is shared with anyone, sold, or used for advertising, and none of it is sent to any AI service.
  • AI Hub's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

In AI Hub, Settings → Email → Disconnect deletes the sign-in from the computer and asks Google to withdraw it. Access can also be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
