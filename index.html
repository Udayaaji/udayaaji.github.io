AI Hub

A personal desktop app for Windows.

AI Hub carries out tasks and scheduled jobs on its owner's own computer, from small ones to multi-step work with Claude or Codex, and can email the results to the owner's own Gmail address.

It is a private, single-user app. It isn't sold or offered to anyone else, and it has no website account or server of its own.

Gmail

AI Hub asks Google for permission to send email from the owner's Gmail account to that same address, to save an email as a draft, and to read one header of sent messages to confirm that an email went. It doesn't read the contents of any email. Details are in the privacy policy.
